Why SKYSHIELD exists

Technology should protect people.

Our aim is to explore practical, responsible ways engineering can help civilian communities stay safer, informed and connected during difficult circumstances.

We are an independent initiative in development, focused on civilian-centred research and humanitarian engineering, with particular interest in resilience and recovery in Ukraine. We believe useful technology starts with listening to communities, understanding real needs and taking safety seriously.

Project SKYSHIELD is currently an independent initiative in development. It is not a registered charity or incorporated organisation.

Areas of interest

Research with a civilian purpose.

Our areas of interest are exploratory. Any future work will be assessed for safety, legality and genuine civilian benefit.
