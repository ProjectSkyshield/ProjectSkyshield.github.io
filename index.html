PROJECT SKYSHIELD

INDEPENDENT INITIATIVE · IN DEVELOPMENT

Engineering a safer future.

Project SKYSHIELD is an independent engineering initiative focused on developing responsible technology for civilian safety, connectivity and humanitarian resilience in Ukraine.

Our Mission

We aim to explore how engineering and technology can contribute to protecting civilian communities and keeping people connected during difficult circumstances.

Research Areas

Passive Sensing & Detection

Exploring non-interfering sensing technologies for civilian safety.

Connectivity Resilience

Investigating ways to support reliable civilian communications.

Humanitarian Engineering

Exploring practical engineering solutions for civilian communities.

Our Principles

Civilian safety first. Preserve connectivity. Responsible engineering. Transparency and accountability.
